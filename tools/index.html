Raspberry Pi 4 Model B boardview

A KiCad reconstruction of the Raspberry Pi 4 Model B board (part placement, part identification, nets traced from real boards), one per board revision. The revision is printed on the board's top silkscreen.

The HTML viewer is self-contained and works offline. The .brd opens in OpenBoardView. Source and corrections: github.com/dpetzel/pi4-boardview.
